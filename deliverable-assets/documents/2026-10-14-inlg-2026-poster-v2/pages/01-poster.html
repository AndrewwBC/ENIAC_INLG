1 Motivation

ABSA extracts each aspect term and its polarity. Portuguese data is scarce, so LLM paraphrases are an appealing way to scale it.

O positivecafé da manhã era ótimo, mas o negativequarto estava sujo.
“The breakfast was great, but the room was dirty.”
ATE · aspect term extractionASC · sentiment
Research question
Does in-context LLM paraphrasing yield data that improves a strong model on Portuguese ABSA?

3 Results

Augmentation never helps: the paired delta is negative in 4 of 5 seeds for every setup. Only the Qwen2.5-7B extraction drop is significant; sentiment stays within seed noise.

4 Why no gain?

Human data is already the closest to the test set. The injected diversity is mostly lexical: no evidence of new semantic coverage, only surface variety that dilutes the training signal.

Take-home message
Diverse paraphrases do not help: what matters is distance to the evaluation distribution, not distance to the seed.

And tuning anchoring, temperature and filters undercuts the appeal of automated LLM data scaling.

Scope & limitations
  • No gain above ≈ 1 F1 point, not a uniform drop: only the Qwen2.5-7B extraction drop is significant (5 seeds, fixed split).
  • One hotel-review corpus, one target model, one augmentation policy (append all kept paraphrases).
  • Hyperparameters fixed a priori, no dev set; two open-weight paraphrasers; cosine and BLEU are imperfect proxies.
