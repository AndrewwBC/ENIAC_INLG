1 Motivation

ABSA extracts each aspect term of an opinion and its polarity. Portuguese data is scarce, so LLM paraphrases are an appealing way to scale it.

O positivecafé da manhã era ótimo, mas o negativequarto estava sujo.
“The breakfast was great, but the room was dirty.”
ATE · aspect term extractionASC · sentiment
Research question
Does in-context LLM paraphrasing yield data that improves a strong model on Portuguese ABSA?

Anchoring prompt keeps aspect terms verbatim; keep p only if cos(t, p) < 0.85  and  BLEU(p, t) < 30

3 Results

Augmentation never helps: extraction drops for both LLMs and sentiment stays within seed noise. The paired delta is negative in 4 of 5 seeds for every configuration.

Take-home message
Diverse paraphrases do not help: what matters is distance to the evaluation distribution, not distance to the seed.
  1. A simple PTT5 baseline beats the best ABSAPT 2024 systems on both sub-tasks.
  2. Diverse, aspect-anchored paraphrases do not improve over it, for either LLM.
  3. The calibration these heuristics need undercuts the appeal of automated LLM data scaling.
